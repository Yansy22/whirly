How this works — tap to read
  1. Add a piece: fill in the name and price, pick a photo, and tap Save piece. It appears on your website straight away.
  2. Name is the pretty title customers see big (like "Starlight you can wear"). Materials is the small line underneath.
  3. Price is what customers pay (in PKR). It shows on the piece and is used for the cart total.
  4. Edit / Remove: use the buttons on any piece below. Leave the photo empty when editing to keep the current one.
Tip · Tall (portrait) photos look best. Customers now add pieces to a cart and check out on the site — new orders show up under the Orders tab.
