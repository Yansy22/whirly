How this works — tap to read
  1. Add a piece: fill in the name, pick a photo, and tap Save piece. It appears on your website straight away.
  2. Name is the pretty title customers see big (like "Starlight you can wear"). Materials is the small line underneath (like "Silver-tone · star · pearl").
  3. Edit: tap Edit on any piece below, change what you like, then Update. Leave the photo empty to keep the current one.
  4. Remove: tap Delete on a piece to take it off the site.
